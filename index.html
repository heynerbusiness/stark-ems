02

Why EMS

The short, honest science — no miracle claims, just a smarter signal.

EMS — electrical muscle stimulation — sends low-frequency impulses to your muscles through electrodes in a fitted suit.

Those impulses mimic the signals your own brain sends. The difference: they reach more muscle fibers, deeper layers, all at the same time — including the deep stabilizing muscles along your spine that conventional training barely reaches. You perform simple, guided movements; the impulse makes every contraction count.

Because the work comes from the contraction itself, there is no need to load your joints with heavy weights. Intensity goes up; joint stress stays down. That is what makes EMS genuinely low-impact — and why one focused 20-minute session, once or twice a week, is a complete program rather than a warm-up.

Straight talk: EMS is a fitness and strength service — not a medical treatment, and not a shortcut around consistency. It works when you show up weekly. We will never promise you anything else.

Safety first

EMS is powerful — which is exactly why it runs on protocol, not vibes. Here is ours, in the open.

How every client starts

  • Health screening first — every engagement begins with a screening and contraindication check, before the suit goes on.
  • Certified trainers only — your session is coached 1-on-1, start to finish. You never train unsupervised.
  • Gradual build-up — intensity starts low and ramps over your first weeks: max. 1 session per week at the start, then up to 2, always with rest days between.
  • You control the dial — intensity is set with you, per muscle group, every session.
  • Hydration & recovery — guidance before and after every session is part of the service.

Who must not train

  • People with a pacemaker or other electronic implants
  • During pregnancy
  • People with epilepsy
  • During acute illness or fever

Have another condition, a recent surgery, or medication you are unsure about? Ask us first — we will tell you honestly whether EMS is right for you, and when in doubt we will ask you to check with your doctor before we start.

Questions, answered

What exactly is EMS?+

EMS stands for electrical muscle stimulation. You wear a fitted suit with electrodes over all major muscle groups. During simple guided movements, low-frequency impulses make your muscles contract — deeper and more completely than voluntary training alone. A certified trainer controls everything, 1-on-1, for 20 minutes.

Does it hurt?+

No. It feels like a strong tingling pressure while your muscles work — unusual the first time, then simply intense. Most clients describe it as intense but comfortable. You and your trainer set the intensity together, per muscle group, and you can turn it down at any moment.

How often should I train?+

Once or twice a week. EMS is a full-body stimulus, so your muscles need at least 48 hours to recover and adapt — that recovery is where the progress happens. In your first weeks we cap it at one session per week while your body gets used to the training.

When will I see results?+

That depends on your starting point, your goal and — above all — consistency, which is why we don't guess. We measure: your strength and body composition are checked at the start and re-checked every 6 weeks, so progress shows up in numbers, not in promises. What we can promise is the structure: one focused session a week, every week.

What do I wear or bring?+

Almost nothing. We bring the suit and your personal undergarment set (included with packs, yours to keep). You need about 2 × 2 meters of space, a water bottle, and 30 minutes of your day. No shoes, no gym bag, no equipment.

Is it safe?+

We train exclusively on FDA-cleared devices, with certified trainers and a health screening before your first session. EMS is not for everyone: it is off-limits with pacemakers or electronic implants, during pregnancy, with epilepsy or acute illness. See the Safety section for the full list — we publish it because we mean it.

Wherever suits you on the Westside — your home, your office, your garden. We currently serve Venice, Santa Monica, Marina del Rey, Brentwood, Pacific Palisades, Beverly Hills, West Hollywood, Culver City, West LA and Manhattan Beach.

Because of what is in the room: a private certified trainer, professional German EMS technology, and a service that comes to your door.
